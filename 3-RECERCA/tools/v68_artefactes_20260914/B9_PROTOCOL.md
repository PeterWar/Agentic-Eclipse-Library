# Fine lunar detector pilot

The remaining stripes lie mostly at wavelengths 4–12 pixels, outside the 12–96 pixel domain of the V55/V56 detector estimate. New bounded hypothesis: a finer detector-fixed component can be separated by the observed native shifts, without suppressing true lunar texture.

Use every Vixen source with exposure >=0.5 s from the frozen 67-frame campaign, the existing calibration and geometry, physical support, per-source total residual variance smoothed at sigma 32, global 1400-square latent field, raised-cosine passband 4–12 with skirts 3–16, ridge 0.1. Divide frames by alternating time within exposure groups; fit both halves and all frames. Only cross-repeatable spectral components may be subtracted. No Sony, LROC, annotations or photo participates in the detector fit. Keep photographic response fitting separate, then compare all previous external retention metrics and injections. No extrapolation to the last limb or PSB promotion without those gates.
